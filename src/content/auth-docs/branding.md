# Branding & Templates

Branding and templates control the tenant-specific identity experience.

## Branding

- Branding template management in the console.
- Public branding lookup for hosted identity screens.
- Public logo route.
- Tenant-specific colors and visual identity.

## Email Templates

Email templates support identity flows such as:

- Email verification.
- Forgot password.
- Magic link.
- Invite.
- MFA.
- Email change.
- Device approval.
- CIBA notification.

## SMS Templates

SMS templates support short-message flows such as login, MFA, and verification.

## Operating Model

Templates are managed through the console, rendered by backend notification flows, and scoped by tenant with fallbacks where configured.
